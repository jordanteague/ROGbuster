A LITTLE LESS HAUNTING. A LOT MORE CLARITY.

From evasive answers to a clear next step.

  1. Upload their responses

    PDF, Word, text or photos. Scanned PDFs are read with OCR. Drop the wrong document and ROGbuster tells you.

  2. Add your requests (optional)

    Add the requests you served and the complaint, so each answer is judged against what you actually asked and what the case is about.

  3. Review the gaps

    Every response gets a 0–5 completeness score and a list of issues. Change the score, X out an issue, add your own, and mark each one reviewed.

  4. Draft the letter

    One click drafts a meet-and-confer letter covering every deficient response, addressed to opposing counsel from their signature block. Or export everything to CSV.

Interrogatory No. 1

Identify each person who performed any work on the system, including installation, commissioning, service or repair, and for each person state the dates and nature of the work performed.

Their response

Frostbyte objects to this Interrogatory as overly broad and unduly burdensome. Subject to and without waiving these objections, Frostbyte states that its technicians installed, commissioned and serviced the system in the ordinary course of business.

1/5Names no one and gives no dates

Issues to raise

  • Doesn't identify any technician by name, or the dates and work each performed.
  • Boilerplate objections with no explanation of the burden.

What to ask for

Identify each person by name and title, with the dates and work each performed.

Who it's for

Lawyers and paralegals who serve written discovery in US litigation and have to chase incomplete answers.

What you need

A Claude account and a browser. Nothing to install, and nothing to pay for.

What it reads

Interrogatories, requests for production and requests for admission, including responses that open with general objections or a cover letter.

Questions

Where do my documents go?

Your files are read in your browser. Their text is sent to Claude, through your own Claude account, to do the analysis. Your work in progress is saved in your browser on that device. ROGbuster has no server of its own. Follow your firm's policies on using AI tools with client material.

Is this legal advice?

No. ROGbuster is a first pass that points you at likely problems. Claude can miss things or get them wrong, so read each response yourself and edit the letter before you send it.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Yes. Scans are read with OCR in your browser, and Claude re-reads any page the OCR struggled with.

Which courts and rules?

It works with US federal and state written discovery. It picks up the court, the parties and both sides' counsel from your documents, and you can correct any of them before drafting the letter.

What does it cost?

Nothing. ROGbuster is free and open source, and you can read every line of it on GitHub. The analysis runs on your own Claude account, so your plan's usage limits apply.
