3. Boilerplate objections. Boilerplate objections, including objections that a request is "overbroad, unduly burdensome, vague and ambiguous" without explanation, are improper. Any objection not stated with particularity will be deemed waived for purposes of a motion to compel.

5. Contention interrogatories. The court will not sustain an objection that a contention interrogatory is "premature." A party must respond with all facts presently known to it and must supplement as discovery proceeds.

6. Requests for admission. A party that responds to a request for admission by claiming lack of information or knowledge must state in the response the reasonable inquiry it made, including the persons consulted and documents reviewed.
